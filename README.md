## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 💳 1. [Bank GoodCredit Credit Risk Scoring](https://github.com/HimanshuParamhansSharma/Bank-GoodCredit-Credit-Risk-Scoring)
> **Predictive Risk Analytics & End-to-End Scoring Engine**[cite: 3]

- **Architecture:** Consolidated 3 raw relational tables to extract bureau-style behavioral features, including delinquency timing, credit utilization trends, and enquiry recency[cite: 3].
- **Modeling & Imbalance:** Built and benchmarked Random Forest and champion **LightGBM Classifiers** utilizing cost-sensitive class-weighting to handle severe target imbalance[cite: 3].
- **Results:** Surpassed the business-agreed **Gini benchmark of 37.90%** and validated risk separation using a **10-decile rank-ordering analysis** for deployment into credit approval pipelines[cite: 3].

### ✈️ 2. [Flight Price Prediction Pipeline](https://github.com/HimanshuParamhansSharma/Flight-Price-Prediction)
> **End-to-End Machine Learning & Analytics Framework**[cite: 2, 3]

- **Data Engineering:** Formatted complex transit records, resolved missing routing parameters via SQL-based imputation, and normalized time attributes[cite: 3].
- **Optimization:** Benchmarked Decision Trees, Random Forest, KNN, and XGBoost; achieved a peak **$R^2$ of 0.88** and reduced RMSE by **14%** through 5-fold cross-validation and hyperparameter tuning[cite: 2, 3].
- **Business Intelligence:** Created an interactive Power BI dashboard visualizing price fluctuations across carrier routes and departure slots for self-service cost optimization[cite: 3].

### 🦠 3. [COVID-19 Global Time-Series Forecasting](https://github.com/HimanshuParamhansSharma/COVID19-Global-Forecasting)
> **Deep Learning Trend Analysis & Projection Engine**[cite: 3]

- **Pipeline Design:** Unpivoted multi-country wide-format tracking data into unified chronological timelines for trend monitoring[cite: 3].
- **Deep Learning Execution:** Evaluated Linear, Polynomial Regression, and Deep Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) using TensorFlow/Keras[cite: 2, 3].
- **Performance:** Achieved a test **RMSE of 53,270** on multi-month projection windows to assist strategic projection pipelines[cite: 2, 3].

### 🏀 4. [NBA Shot Selection Prediction](https://github.com/HimanshuParamhansSharma/NBA-Shot-Selection-Prediction)
> **Spatial & Situational Classification Pipeline**[cite: 2]
